# Weekday recurring alarms (Step 7A)

An enabled alarm with selected weekdays schedules one exact regular occurrence with `AlarmManager.setAlarmClock()`. When the receiver accepts that occurrence, it consumes its persisted identity once and immediately schedules the next selected weekday. Dismiss, timeout and the temporary simultaneous-alarm skip policy complete only the delivered occurrence. The repeat definition remains enabled. A stale or duplicate intent cannot advance the rule again.

The regular pending occurrence, currently ringing occurrence and pending five-minute snooze have separate persisted IDs, revisions and timestamps. Snooze uses an elapsed-realtime five-minute deadline within the same boot, plus a wall-clock recovery instant for reboot, and never moves the next regular weekday. Cancelling a recurring snooze removes only the snooze entry. A one-shot still becomes disabled when its final ringing session ends or its snooze is cancelled. Disable, delete and explicit schedule saves invalidate the old persisted identities before cancelling their platform entries.

The existing `NextOccurrenceCalculator` resolves local wall time. A daylight-saving gap shifts the alarm forward by the gap duration; an overlap chooses the earlier offset and rings once on that date. After a late delivery, the next calculation starts after the later of the delivery's scheduled instant and current time. Missed intermediate days are not replayed. Time-zone, manual-clock, reboot and app-update reconciliation were added in Step 7B.

Room migration 3→4 adds nullable snooze and ringing slots. It moves pending one-shot snoozes and ringing identities into those slots without deleting alarms or audio. Previously saved `UNSUPPORTED_RECURRING` definitions become `NOT_SCHEDULED` while retaining their configured intent; they receive no system occurrence during upgrade, so the user must explicitly Save or enable them. If scheduling the next repeat fails, the alarm remains configured as enabled and the list shows that the next repeat is not scheduled. Reboot and clock-change recovery is implemented in Step 7B; see [schedule recovery](alarm-recovery.md).
